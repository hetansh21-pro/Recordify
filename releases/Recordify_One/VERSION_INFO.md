## Included Features
1. **Full Spotify Dark Theme**: Edge-to-edge `#121212` UI, Spotify Green `#1DB954` accents, Spotify rounded cards.
2. **Playlist Management & Liked Songs**:
   - Spotify "+ / Checkmark" button in full-screen player.
   - Add to Liked Songs (purple gradient tile) and custom playlists bottom sheet.
   - Create new playlists from player or library header.
   - Playlist detail view with song list, playback, rename, delete, and song removal.
3. **Multi-Artist Separation & Discography**:
   - Automatic separation of collaborating artists into individual artist pages.
   - Artist discography view with popular songs list and hero banner.
4. **Horizontal Swipe Lock**:
   - Locks horizontal tab swiping when viewing artist pages or playlist detail pages.
5. **Smooth Animated Bottom Navigation Bar**:
   - Sliding green indicator pill with spring animation and scaling active icons.
6. **Redesigned Modern Dark Settings Screen**:
   - Segmented pill buttons for AAC Bitrates and Silence Timeouts.
   - On-device privacy assurance and storage statistics.
7. **Cover Art & Artist Pictures**:
   - In-app cached online art fetching for covers and artist pictures.
